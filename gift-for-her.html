Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy an O.G. Nails gift card?

Gift cards are sold online through Vagaro, the same system Olga uses for booking. Choose the amount, pay online and send it to her. If you have any trouble, message Olga on Instagram @o.g_nails.

How much should I put on the gift card?

$120 covers a gel manicure, $260 a gel manicure and gel pedicure, and $290 adds the Hand Spa Treatment. If she wears hard gel or extensions, $140 to $180 covers a gel fill. Full prices are on the Services page.

Can she choose a different service?

Yes. A gift card is a dollar amount, so she can put it towards whichever service she prefers, from a gel manicure to an advanced pedicure.

I have no idea what colour or style she likes. Is that a problem?

Not at all. She chooses the colour and design on the day, and Olga helps her decide. All you need to do is pick the amount.

Can I book the appointment for her as a surprise?

Yes. Message Olga on Instagram @o.g_nails with the date you have in mind and she will help you arrange it.
