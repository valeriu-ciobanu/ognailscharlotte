What it is

More than a pedicure, gentler than you expect.

Advanced Pedicure and Foot Care is for feet that need more than polish. Olga combines careful nail work and dry e-file skin care to make feet look better and feel more comfortable, without soaking and without rushing.

It's equally suited to women and men, and to anyone who finds their feet hard to look after on their own.

Common concerns we address

  • Thick toenails
  • Dry and cracked heels
  • Calluses and corns
  • Early ingrown toenails
  • Nail trimming for difficult nails
  • General foot maintenance

We do not diagnose or treat fungal infections. If a medical condition is suspected, we recommend consulting a podiatrist.

The process

What happens at your appointment.

Every visit follows the same careful steps, adjusted to what your feet need on the day.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Olga looks at your feet and nails, talks through any concerns and confirms your service level, so you know the price before the service begins.

  2. 02

    Nail care

    Careful trimming, shaping and thinning of thick or difficult nails, plus gentle attention to the nail folds and early ingrown edges.

  3. 03

    Skin care

    Dry (waterless) e-file work to reduce calluses, corns and hard skin and to smooth dry, cracked heels, focused on your problem areas.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Cuticle care, moisturizer and a long-lasting gel polish, with simple advice for keeping your feet comfortable between visits.

Results · Heels & skin

Before & after.

Real results from the studio. Some require multiple visits and a long recovery. This is cosmetic foot care, not medical treatment; for complex cases, see a podiatrist.

Ingrown & curved toenails

Titanium wire toenail correction.

For toenails that curve into the skin, a thin titanium wire is fitted across the nail to gently lift the edges as it grows. A gentle, non-invasive option for nails that are starting to curve in.

Considering surgery? Book an Advanced Pedicure first.

Before visiting a podiatrist for surgical treatment, book an Advanced Pedicure to get a consultation. Olga will check whether titanium wire correction could solve the problem in a gentler way.

Suitable for early ingrown or curved nails. Infected or painful ingrown toenails should be seen by a podiatrist first.
