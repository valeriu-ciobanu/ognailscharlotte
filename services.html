The technique

What is a dry, or Russian, manicure?

A dry manicure is performed without soaking. Instead of softening skin in water, Olga uses a precision e-file and fine instruments to gently lift and remove dead cuticle tissue and refine the nail fold.

Because the nail isn’t swollen with water, polish is applied to its true shape. That lets colour sit closer to the cuticle, look cleaner, and keep its finish for longer. The same careful approach is used for pedicures.

Why clients choose it

  • Cleaner cuticle line. Precise removal of dead skin for a polished, groomed look.
  • Longer-lasting finish. Polish applied to a dry nail keeps its true shape.
  • Health-focused. Strengthening options, and gel only on a suitable natural nail.
  • One-on-one. Time to get every detail right.
