About Olga

Hi, I’m Olga. Welcome to my studio.

I began my career in 2017 and spent eight years building my expertise and caring for clients in Washington State before bringing that experience to Charlotte.

What I love most is the detail: a perfectly clean cuticle line, a shape that suits your hands, and nails that stay healthy appointment after appointment. I keep learning, so you always get the most refined technique I can offer.
